# How to write an article for FRHS.tech
Either low on CSHS hours, or have some cool thing you want to share?
  
## Projects
Projects are somthing computer science-related project you have worked on, and you want to share with people.
  
Please include the following in the content of your article:  
- **Description**: What you made does, and why you made it.  
- **Process**: How your project works, or anything else you want to mention  
- **Review**: What you thought about your project  

## Others
We are open to any type of computer-science related article, like writups about various tools and concepts, or anything else. Feel free to email about some new format for an article.

## Submitting
  
Please submit the article in Markdown format (.md), Google Docs has the ability to export a document in Markdown, or you could convert it yourself (Markdown is not complex)  
  
For inclusion on the website, also include:  
- **Title**: The short title you want your project known as  
- **Subtitle**: A very short description of your project  
- **Author**: the author(s) that worked on the article  
- **Image (optional)**: A prefferably 3x1 aspect ratio image to accompany your project  
- **Links**: Any additional links, like Scratch, GitHub, website link, or refrences that you want to include
  
Send an email to [cshs@frhs.tech](mailto:cshs@frhs.tech) with the attached markdown file, along with any questions

## Hours
We will give CSHS hours for every hour spend on the actual project or article, within reason. Talk to Mr. Ruffer for more details.
